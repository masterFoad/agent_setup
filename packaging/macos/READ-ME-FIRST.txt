FOAD Dev Setup — read this first
================================

1. Double-click  "FOAD Dev Setup"  in this window to start.

2. Verify the DMG's SHA-256 value against the checksum published by your
   instructor. If it does not match exactly, stop and delete the DMG.

3. If macOS says it cannot verify the developer, do not bypass the warning
   unless your instructor confirms that this exact checksum is the expected
   supervised workshop build. Public releases should be signed and notarized.

4. macOS may ask for your Mac password while Homebrew installs. That is
   expected — type your login password and press Return.

5. When it finishes, close and reopen Terminal, then run:  claude

The other files (install-mac.sh, README.md) are for reference and for
advanced users. You only need to double-click "FOAD Dev Setup".
